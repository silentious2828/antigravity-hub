## Post 2: Global Export Scale & Margin Protection

**3 Lessons from Managing 120+ Monthly Export Shipments Across 8 Global Markets**

Scaling international distribution across high-friction corridors in APAC, Europe, Africa, and the Middle East requires more than competitive pricing. It demands operational rigor:

1. **Dealer Incentive Alignment:** A 25% expansion in dealer networks only translates to margin growth if local distributor incentives match regional inventory velocity.

2. **Inventory Precision:** Leveraging SAP MM material management rules eliminates stockout friction before goods leave the warehouse.

3. **Proactive Trade Compliance:** Zero-defect trade and customs documentation prevents costly port demurrage.

When commercial leadership is paired with modern digital supply chain tools, gross margins remain protected even during market volatility.
