# 03_Areas - Areas of Responsibility

## Purpose
- Areas of your life/work that you're responsible for and want to maintain
- Unlike projects, areas have no end date - they're ongoing
- Examples: Health, Career, Finance, Relationships, This AI SaaS Project
- Review periodically to ensure they're being nurtured

## Area Categories
- Health & Wellness
- Professional Development
- Financial
- Relationships
- This AI SaaS Enterprise project
