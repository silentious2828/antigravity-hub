**Time to first dollar:** 1–7 days  
**Startup cost:** S$0–200  
**Revenue ceiling:** S$5K–15K/mo  
**Your unfair advantage:** You already have the templates, the CV variants, the dossier system.

**What you sell:**
- AI-powered CV optimization for supply chain, SAP, logistics professionals
- "I'll transform your generic CV into an ATS-optimized, SAP-MM-tagged, AI-workflow-ready document."
- Price: S$50–200 per document, or S$500–1,000 for a full package (CV + cover letter + LinkedIn)

**Why this works fast:**
- You already have the product (the SSOT CV system)
- High urgency need (job seekers)

**Why it's not the main play:**

- Harder to scale to 24/7 without more templates
